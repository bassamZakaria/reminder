# Recall Support

## Getting started

1. Open Recall and tap **Create an account**. Confirm your email from the link we send you, then sign in.
2. Tap **＋** on the **Reminders** tab to add a reminder, or on the **Notes** tab to write a note.
3. A note can have several reminders. Open a note and tap **＋ Add** under Reminders.

## Reminders don't alert me

- Open **Settings** in Recall and check that **Notifications** says **Allowed**. If it says **Off**, turn notifications on in your device settings.
- Tap **Send a test notification** in Settings to check alerts on that device.
- On iPhone, an **Alarm** reminder rings like the Clock app on iOS 26 and later. On earlier versions it arrives as a time-sensitive notification.
- On Android, allow **Alarms & reminders** for Recall when asked, so reminders fire on time.

## Sync

Changes appear on your other signed-in devices within a few seconds. Pull down on a list to refresh.

## Delete your account

**Settings › Delete Account** permanently deletes your account and all of your data.
